# Polymarket's 15-Minute Contracts Hit $1B in Six Weeks, and Oil and Gas Are Next

*Short-dated "up or down" markets are the fastest-growing product line in the category. Kalshi walks away from one Louisiana suit, and a Fourth Circuit panel sides with Maryland.*

**Musashi Daily · Thursday, November 19, 2026** · Polymarket Gamma API checked 00:00 UTC (08:00 China time; 7:00 PM ET Nov 18). Polymarket volumes are USD, 24h. Context for builders, not betting or financial advice.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. Polymarket US: $1B+ in short-dated contracts in six weeks.** Crypto "up/down" contracts (5-minute to 4-hour windows) passed $1B in notional with 300,000+ traders. Polymarket US is adding crude oil and natural gas, listed alongside gold and silver (15-minute and hourly), no earlier than Nov 19 ([TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/business/60893)). Ticker Tracker puts November crypto up/down at $931M, 10.3% of platform volume, versus just $4.3M for commodities so far. 币圈网 summarises it: "大宗商品仍处早期阶段" ([币圈网](https://www.alibtc.com/zixun/qkl/1596653.html)). *Why builders care:* the product with the most traction is not a headline event market. It is a fast-settling price contract, and commodities are untested.

**2. Kalshi's 15-minute gold beat its ether twin.** In its first month Kalshi's gold contract traded 542M contracts (est. $5M fees) vs 318M and $2.6M for ether ([GTokenTool](https://www.gtokentool.com/news/6110)). *Why builders care:* fee revenue per contract, not just volume, is the metric to design around.

**3. Kalshi drops its Western District of Louisiana suit.** Voluntary dismissal without prejudice on Tuesday, days before a Nov 23 hearing on its injunction motion. The other Louisiana cases, including the CFTC's, are consolidated before Judge Africk and continue ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/kalshi-voluntarily-dismisses-louisiana-murrill-lawsuit-western-district)). New angle on the Louisiana thread: the platform chose the federal-government-led track.

**4. Fourth Circuit leaves Maryland's enforcement in place.** A 2-1 panel affirmed the denial of Kalshi's injunction in a one-line per curiam; the two majority judges took different routes ([iGaming Times](https://igaming-times.com/news/prediction-markets)). *Why builders care:* circuit splits mean state-by-state product availability will keep changing. Build geofencing as configuration, not code.

**5. Kalshi to list AI-compute futures with Compute Desk.** Plans cover GPU-rental price markets first, then perpetuals referencing the Ornn benchmark and dated futures on Compute Desk's index. CFTC has pushed a related approval decision to early 2027 ([数链库](https://shulianku.com/2026/11/19/%e9%a2%84%e6%b5%8b%e5%b8%82%e5%9c%ba%e5%b9%b3%e5%8f%b0kalshi%e6%8b%9f%e6%8e%a8ai%e7%ae%97%e5%8a%9b%e6%9c%9f%e8%b4%a7%ef%bc%8c%e4%b8%8ecompute-desk%e5%90%88%e4%bd%9c%e5%bc%80%e5%8f%91gpu%e8%a1%8d/)). This ties to the $65B raise talks reported Nov 12, which Reuters says would fund a compute-futures platform ([Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/kalshi-in-talks-to-raise-money-at-dollar65-billion-valuation-bloomberg-news-reports-5023255)).

**6. Institutions can post BTC as Kalshi margin.** Anchorage Digital and Kalshi Prime let institutional clients pledge BTC and stablecoins held at Anchorage as margin without moving them, via a tri-party custody arrangement ([币链网](https://www.chainbcoin.com/xin-ju-zhong-ji-bi-lei-he-gui)). *Why builders care:* capital efficiency for funds is now a product feature.

**7. Chainlink pricing on new Polymarket crypto markets.** New hourly and daily crypto markets resolve on Chainlink (60-second average); the Nov 18 daily market still used Binance rules ([BIBIBI](https://bibibi.cc/topics/%E9%A2%84%E6%B5%8B%E5%B8%82%E5%9C%BA)). Resolution sources are a product decision.

## Build This

1. **A short-window contract analytics terminal.** Per-venue fee, spread and volume for 5/15/60-minute contracts across crypto, gold, oil. *Why now:* Kalshi and Polymarket US both report real traction here and oil/gas launch today.
2. **Compute-price market data.** An index-aware dashboard for GPU-rental price markets. *Why now:* Kalshi has announced the plan with Compute Desk and the CFTC decision lands early 2027.
3. **Jurisdiction-aware routing layer.** One API that maps users to the venue and contract set allowed where they live, with court-order logs. *Why now:* Louisiana, Maryland, Utah and the CFTC geofence directive all pull in different directions.
4. **Resolution-source auditor.** Flags markets whose rules diverge between Binance, Chainlink and other oracles. *Why now:* Polymarket's daily and hourly crypto markets run on different sources.
5. **Margin-in-kind tooling for funds.** Reporting for pledged BTC/stablecoin collateral across prediction venues. *Why now:* the Anchorage–Kalshi Prime arrangement.
