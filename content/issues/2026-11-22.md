# Kalshi's Institutions Hit $26.3B a Month, and a New York Judge Decides Monday Whether It Can Keep Trading

*Kalshi's institutional volume is up 4,124% in six months, 44 attorneys general push back on the CFTC, courts in Louisiana and Missouri reset the schedule, and the CFTC freezes Trump "mention" markets.*

Checked Nov 21, 2026, ~23:59 UTC / Nov 22, 07:59 China time. Items from prior briefs appear only with new developments.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. Kalshi institutional volume: $26.3B in October.** Up from $622.7M in April (+4,124%), per company figures given to Fortune. 822 institutional accounts have traded (312 in April); 242 trade over $1M a month. Figures exclude market makers and perpetuals. Sports and combo contracts were about 85% of Kalshi volume in the 30 days to Nov 20, finance and economics under 1% (Ticker Tracker, face value). A UCD economist says growth "appears to mainly be funds (and sportsbooks) offering parlay bets to retail takers" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/21/kalshi-institutional-trading-hedging/)). *Builders:* the institutional demand is real, but it is parlay-heavy, and hedging tools (Greenlight brokered trades of $25K to $7M) are still a sliver.

**2. New York hearing is Monday morning.** Judge Schofield denied the CFTC's second emergency bid Friday; a state court now decides Monday on an order Kalshi says would shut its exchange nationwide ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-21-kalshi-faces-monday-hearing-on-new-york-order-it-says-would-shut-down-its-exchange-nationwide-444813)). Gambity notes the order's HQ-based language would not need to name Polymarket to reach it ([Gambity](https://gambity.com/article/intelligence/polymarket-faces-new-york-shutdown-risk-as-hq-clause-tightens-tro-analiz)). *Builders:* the outcome is a binary event for anyone built on one venue.

**3. 44 attorneys general challenge the CFTC.** A bipartisan coalition, co-led by New Jersey AG Jennifer Davenport, filed objections this week to two CFTC rulemakings, arguing platforms should not dodge state gambling law by calling bets financial contracts ([Crypto Times, Nov 22](https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/11/22/44-state-attorneys-general-challenge-cftcs-prediction-market-rules/)).

**4. Louisiana and Missouri courts reset.** In New Orleans, Judge Africk denied all pending TRO motions and ordered joint preliminary-injunction briefing, with a hearing targeted for January or February 2027 ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/africk-order-louisiana-prediction-market-cases-tro-denied-new-briefing)). In St. Louis, Judge Perry coordinated the Kalshi, Polymarket US, Robinhood and Novig-operator (Ludlow) suits against Missouri without consolidating them; a conference is Tuesday ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/perry-coordinates-missouri-prediction-market-suits-hanaway-kalshi-novig)).

**5. CFTC stays Kalshi's Trump "mention" markets.** Kalshi paused contracts on what Trump will say, after the CFTC stayed their certification as "susceptible to manipulation" ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/kalshi-pauses-trump-mention-markets-cftc-stay-certification)). *Builders:* mention markets are a manipulation-surveillance problem now.

**6. Insider-trading bill.** Reps. Casten and Lieu introduced H.R. 11092 Thursday, with whistleblower awards, referred to House Judiciary ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/casten-lieu-hr-11092-prediction-market-insider-trading-whistleblower-awards)).

**7. Polymarket top events, ~23:59 UTC (Gamma API).** Bitcoin 2026 price ladder $1.78M 24h volume; Fed December $1.77M; Bitcoin November $1.23M; Israel x Iran ceasefire $0.90M; Republican 2028 nominee $0.76M. Sports fill the rest of the top 12 ([Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?order=volume24hr&ascending=false&closed=false&limit=12)).
