## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. One game, $126.28M.** DeFi Rate's AllTop-based count puts NFL Week 10 at **$186.84M** across Kalshi, Polymarket US and Polymarket, and Commanders at Giants alone at **$126.28M**, 90% of it on Kalshi ($113.98M). Kalshi carries 70% of the week; 14 games, 3.5M trades. Kalshi combo buyers put in at least $74.80M, averaging 7.8 legs per combo ([DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/odds/nfl/week-10/), data as of Nov 15, 6:37 PM ET). *Why builders care:* one venue owns the sports flow, while Polymarket leads on the Sunday slate. On Polymarket, 49ers vs. Cowboys did ~$8.30M in 24h, the top event at check ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/nfl-sf-dal-2026-11-15)).

**2. Louisiana moves to federal court.** Rothera Exchange (the Robinhood-Susquehanna JV) moved to dissolve the TRO and asked for oral argument today, Nov 16. It follows Robinhood's own motion and Polymarket's removal to federal court; the case sits with Judge Nannette Jolivette Brown ([Adjacent](https://press.adj.news/p/adjacents-look-ahead-week-of-november-6d8)). Kalshi told Louisiana users "your government says you can't use Kalshi anymore," while the Times-Picayune found trading still open ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/kalshi-louisiana-users-notice-your-government-says-tro)).

**3. Kalshi's perps hit $5B.** Gate News: Kalshi's equity perpetual contracts have traded **$5B** since Nov 5, with Nvidia above $100M in a day ([Gate](https://www.gate.com/zh/news/detail/prediction-market-platforms-polymarket-kalshi-see-trading-volume-surge-to-25647079), related headlines). The same page cites Yonhap Infomax: H1 2026 volume at Polymarket and Kalshi reached **$187B**, with a roughly $1.9B election-contract tally.

**4. Europe asks questions.** ESMA says Polymarket and Kalshi lack the authorisations usually needed to sell event contracts in the EU, and asked why restricted-country lists omit some member states ([ForkLog via DCNS](https://www.dcns.ru/news/3287)). Polymarket restricts Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands; Kalshi lists eight EU states.

**5. Kalshi's new-listing firehose.** 241 markets across 35 events launched in 24 hours to Nov 15, 8:30 AM ET, plus 744 recurring brackets. Examples: next NVIDIA CEO, Anthropic's share of Vercel requests ([Prediction News Network](https://www.predictionnewsnetwork.com/p/kalshi-daily-recap-2026-11-15)). *Why builders care:* listing velocity is exactly what the CFTC's self-certification probe targets.

**6. Novig is #3.** Fortune reports Novig is now the third-largest prediction market site ([Fortune, via Adjacent](https://press.adj.news/p/adjacents-look-ahead-week-of-november-6d8)). A NY judge denied its bid to block state enforcement, while Missouri paused action against Kalshi ([NEXTpredict](https://nextpredict.io/market-news/legal/missouri-pauses-kalshi-enforcement/)).

**7. Search share flips.** Blask data: Kalshi holds **51.89%** of US prediction-market search demand in November vs Polymarket's 45.19%, but Polymarket leads year to date, 68.2% to 27.54% ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/most-popular-prediction-markets-november-2026)).
