# Wall Street Plugs In: LSEG Takes 25,000 Kalshi Markets, Talos Takes Polymarket

*Institutional rails arrived for both big venues in one day, Coinbase plans its own exchange, and a Utah judge hears the preemption fight this afternoon.*

Checked Nov 20, 2026, ~00:00 UTC / 08:00 China time. Items from prior briefs appear only with new developments.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. Kalshi data lands in LSEG Workspace.** Institutional users get real-time probabilities for 25,000+ event outcomes across seven categories. Kalshi cites 250+ institutional firms and a 5,000% rise in institutional notional volume ([Tradetech Eye, Nov 19](https://tradetecheye.com/news/lseg-brings-25-000-kalshi-markets-into-workspace-eadc4d0f)). *Builders:* the buyer is now the terminal desk, so normalized data, entitlements and analytics are the product.

**2. Polymarket's international exchange goes live on Talos.** Eligible non-US institutions can trade through an OEMS with algos, position management and risk controls, the first onchain prediction platform in such a system ([NextPredict, Nov 19](https://nextpredict.io/market-news/industry/talos-polymarket-institutional-trading-platform/)). Chinese coverage flags the open question: "落地规模仍待观察" (actual scale remains to be seen) ([BiRun/Foresight News](https://bi.run/newsflash/118463)). Talos separately published a simulation across three Kalshi markets claiming slippage costs and a possible settlement-manipulation pattern ([Talos](https://www.talos.com/insights/prediction-markets-are-going-institutional---execution-should-too)).

**3. Coinbase plans its own event contracts for H1 2027.** Bloomberg, via Investing.com, says Coinbase will move in-house instead of sharing revenue with Kalshi, building on its own CFTC-approved clearinghouse and the Dec 2025 deal for The Clearing Company ([Investing.com 中文](https://cn.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/article-3677635)). A major distributor of Kalshi flow is becoming a competitor.

**4. Kalshi's on-demand contracts via API.** Traders generate a contract from a template (weather first, concerts next), then request quotes by RFQ; market makers have three seconds to confirm ([FXBrokerTrust](https://www.fxbrokertrust.com/zh-cn/insights/8341)). API users only; these contracts are excluded from normal listings.

**5. CFTC v. Utah hearing today.** Judge Robert Shelby hears the US/CFTC request for a temporary restraining order against Utah's criminal and civil cases at 1:30 p.m. MST Nov 20 ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/cftc-utah-tro-hearing-judge-shelby-prediction-markets-nov-20)). New since the Maryland ruling: Polymarket US and OG.com sued Arkansas ([Dimers](https://www.dimers.com/prediction-markets/legal/polymarket-us-and-og-com-sue-arkansas-to-block-prediction-market-enforcement)).

**6. Polymarket US files for oil and gas.** QCX filed a self-certification Nov 17 for WTI, Brent and Henry Hub binaries (e.g. WTI above $100, Brent above $110, Dec 8 2:30 pm ET). Short-dated crypto reportedly passed $1B in six weeks with 300,000+ traders ([EdgeX/CoinCentral](https://pro.edgex.exchange/zh-CN/news/article/polymarket-us-oil-natural-gas-contracts)).

**7. Kalshi dropping Trump-remark contracts; Quintenz defends the model.** WSJ reports Kalshi will stop contracts on what Trump says on given days ([Sell The News/WSJ](https://sellthenews.org/zh-cn/catalyx/news/wsj-articles-kalshi-to-halt-bets-that-are-based-on-trumps-remarks-fb481bdb3a0968cc)). Kalshi board member Brian Quintenz said insider trading is "just easier to see" in prediction markets, and former commissioners urged clearer CFTC guidance on "manipulable" contracts ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-11-19-kalshi-quintenz-insider-trading-prediction-markets-stocks-444133)).

## Build This

1. **Institutional entitlement and normalization layer.** One schema, cross-venue contract mapping, audit trails for desks that now see Kalshi in LSEG and Polymarket in Talos. *Why now:* both landed Nov 19.
2. **Execution-quality analytics (TCA) for event contracts.** Slippage, book depth and settlement-window monitoring. *Why now:* Talos is publishing its own TCA on Kalshi.
3. **On-demand contract RFQ client.** SDK and UI for Kalshi's template-to-RFQ flow, starting with weather hedges for operators. *Why now:* API-only today.
4. **Venue-switching tooling for Coinbase-style distributors.** Routing and settlement abstraction so a distributor can change liquidity partner. *Why now:* Coinbase's 2027 plan.
5. **Court-calendar and enforcement tracker.** Today's Utah hearing, Louisiana intervention (Dec 9 submission), Maryland, Arkansas, mapped to contract availability by state. *Why now:* circuit split.
