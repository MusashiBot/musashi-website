# New York Moves to Shut Kalshi Down as the Senate Passes the First Prediction-Market Bill

*A federal judge sends Kalshi's home-state case back to state court and refuses the CFTC's emergency bid, the Senate bans war and death contracts, and a combat-sports prediction startup raises $3M.*

Checked Nov 21, 2026, ~00:01 UTC / 08:01 China time. Items from prior briefs appear only with new developments.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. New York can now seek a Kalshi stop order.** On Nov 19 a Manhattan federal court remanded New York's case against Kalshi to state court; the Attorney General then filed a proposed temporary restraining order motion ([CoinDesk, Nov 20](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/11/20/kalshi-races-to-get-ahead-of-new-york-order-that-could-shut-down-exchange)). The CFTC argued such an order would force Kalshi to "stop all operations" because it is headquartered in New York. On Friday Judge Lorna Schofield refused the CFTC's emergency request, saying it had "not established a likelihood of success on the merits"; she noted three appeals courts have sided with states and one against ([Reuters via StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/US+judge+rejects+CFTC+emergency+bid+to+block+New+York+from+policing+Kalshi/27382726.html)). Chinese coverage: "短期经营不确定性上升" (short-term operating uncertainty rises) ([BiRun](https://bi.run/newsflash/119941)). *Builders:* venue concentration and geofence tooling are now real operational risks.

**2. Senate passes the DEATH BETS Act.** The first standalone federal prediction-market bill to clear a chamber would bar CFTC-registered exchanges from listing contracts on war, terrorism, assassination, US military operations, or outcomes that principally depend on an individual's death. It passed by voice vote Nov 19 and goes to the House ([NextPredict](https://nextpredict.io/market-news/industry/pm-weekly-senate-cftc-kalshi/); [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2026/11/20/senate-passes-first-ever-prediction-market-bill-as-democrats-turn-up-heat-on-upstart-industry/)). An amendment leaves sports and state-law litigation untouched. *Builders:* event-category compliance filters are a feature, not an afterthought.

**3. Pew: midterms drew $2.7B in trading.** Using The Block's data, Pew puts 2026 midterm trading on prediction markets at $2.7B versus $8.5B for the 2024 elections ([Pew, Nov 20](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/11/20/how-the-2026-midterms-played-out-on-kalshi-and-polymarket/)). Election flow is lumpy; sports, crypto and macro have to carry the off-season.

**4. Polymarket International by the numbers.** DeFi Rate data cited by SpazioCrypto: $5.3B notional in October, $80.3B for 2026 so far, versus a $11.3B notional peak in June ([SpazioCrypto](https://zh.spaziocrypto.com/shi-chang/polymarket-talos-jigou-yuce-shichang/)). On a dollars-spent basis October was $2.6B. Notional (each contract counted at $1) overstates cash volume, so check which basis a metric uses.

**5. Polymarket's bitcoin-downside repricing.** Per Longbridge, "Bitcoin dips to $60,000 in November" rose to 72% in 24 hours (from about 43%), and "dips to $60,000 in 2026" stood at 86% ([Longbridge](https://longbridge.com/zh-CN/news/306255803)). AiCoin later quoted 56.35% for a $55,000 touch as of 04:23 Beijing time ([AiCoin](https://www.aicoin.com/zh-Hans/news-flash/3202137)).

**6. Anthropic valuation market cools.** "Anthropic valuation hits $1.75T by Dec 31" fell to 26%, down 25.5 points in 24h, on $153K volume ([Longbridge](https://longbridge.com/zh-CN/news/306255803)).

**7. Polymarket top events, 00:01 UTC (Gamma API).** Fed December decision $4.10M 24h volume (top outcome 99.65%); Israel x Iran ceasefire $1.39M; 2028 Democratic nominee $1.02M ([Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?order=volume24hr&ascending=false&closed=false&limit=12)). Sports fill most of the rest of the top 12.
