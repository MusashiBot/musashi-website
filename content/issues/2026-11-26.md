# Polymarket Prices GPUs Out to 2028, Then Cuts Staff as 1789 Capital Draws a Congressional Probe

*A forward curve for AI compute goes live, Polymarket's $21B round meets a Raskin letter, and New York puts a $52.5M loss number on the table.*

*Checked Nov 26, 2026, 00:00-08:00 China time (Nov 25, 16:00 UTC onward). Context for builders, not betting advice.*

## Today in Prediction Markets

- **Polymarket launches public AI-compute markets (Nov 25).** Cash-settled contracts on Nvidia H100, B200 and B300 hourly rental prices run out to December 2028, referencing the Ornn price index. Polymarket's "implied rental price" curve put the H100 at $3.03 for Dec 2026, $2.67 for Dec 2027 and $2.16 for Dec 2028, against a current index of $3.25. CEO Shayne Coplan: "compute could become one of the largest financial markets in the world." The launch is international only; Polymarket US has not listed compute yet, and CME's compute futures review is extended to Jan 8, 2027. Builders: the first real "yield curve" product in the category ([NEXTPredict](https://nextpredict.io/market-news/tech-and-ai/polymarket-ai-compute-markets/), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/polymarket-ai-compute-pricing-contracts-2028)).
- **Polymarket reportedly raising $1B at $21B, led by 1789 Capital.** WSJ-sourced reports say the Trump Jr.-linked firm may put in $300M on top of an earlier $200M. Rep. Jamie Raskin demanded records from Polymarket and three other companies, saying a $21B valuation could mean a 4,900% return for 1789. The round is unconfirmed ([Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/polymarket-faces-congressional-scrutiny-over-trump-jr-s-investment-gains), [ForkLog via DCNS](https://www.dcns.ru/news/2780)).
- **Polymarket is cutting staff** while Kalshi widens its lead; Bloomberg says the company confirmed "letting some people go" ([Prediction Authority](https://predictionauthority.com/news/polymarket-layoffs-restructuring-kalshi-competition-ipo-2026/)). Kalshi took 51.8% of US brand-search demand in October vs Polymarket's 37.5%, and was the top US Finance and Sports app by downloads Nov 1-23 ([NEXTPredict](https://nextpredict.io/market-news/industry/kalshi-overtakes-polymarket-brand-search-october/)).
- **NYC Council puts numbers on consumer harm.** Speaker Julie Menin says NYC individual users lost about $52.5M net in 2026, with 18-20 year-olds bearing about $8.9M (17% of net losses on ~4% of daily volume). Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase, Gemini and Novig are asked to testify Dec 14. Kalshi disputes the data, saying LLC accounts counted as "institutional" skew it ([igaming-times](https://igaming-times.com/news/prediction-markets); 區塊鏈周刊: "Kalshi 反驳称，这些数字具有误导性", i.e. Kalshi calls the figures misleading, [source](https://chainweeks.com/news/182017.html)).
- **Midterm winners were few.** An ACDC analysis of ~82,000 Polymarket accounts found the top 1% took 91% of midterm profits (~$23M) while the bottom 99% lost ~$15M ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/prediction-markets-polymarket-kalshi-trader-losses/)). Polymarket listed 1,383 midterm events and 13,000+ markets for ~$500M volume ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/podcasts/prediction-market-news/2026-11-25-how-polymarket-priced-the-midterms-446108)).
- **Fed hike market is the liquidity story.** After core PCE came in at 3.3% vs 3.2% expected, Polymarket's Dec 8-9 25bp-hike odds reached 94% (Nov 25). "Fed Decision in December?" had ~$5.4M 24h volume when checked, second only to a "new NVIDIA CEO" market (~$5.4M) ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/polymarket-94-odds-december-fed-rate-hike), [Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?order=volume24hr&ascending=false&closed=false&limit=12)).
- **Lakers sign Kalshi** as official prediction-market sponsor, after the 76ers and the NBA's own deal; terms undisclosed ([Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2026/11/25/la-lakers-announce-deal-with-kalshi-signaling-sports-leagues-growing-embrace-of-prediction-markets/)).
