# The CFTC Pulls Trump "Mention" Markets as Robinhood's Event Revenue Hits $173M

*A quarter of event contracts made Robinhood $173M, Polymarket says it runs at $1.8B a year, and Pump.fun just put Polymarket inside a memecoin app.*

**Musashi Daily · Wednesday, November 18, 2026** · Polymarket Gamma API checked 00:00 UTC (08:00 China time; 7:00 PM ET Nov 17). Polymarket volumes are USD, 24h. Context for builders, not betting or financial advice.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. The CFTC stays Kalshi's Trump "mention" contracts.** Kalshi paused several markets on what Trump will say (nicknames before 2027, topics in November, countries and companies by month-end) at the CFTC's request. The agency said it stayed the certifications "because they may be susceptible to manipulation." One was self-certified in Feb 2025. A Vance DealBook mention market stayed live ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/kalshi-takes-down-trump-mention-markets.html)). 财联社 ran it: "应美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）的要求，撤下了与'特朗普提及'相关的预测市场" ([CLS](https://api3.cls.cn/b/2537191)). *Why builders care:* the regulator is now choosing which contract types survive. Contract design is a compliance question.

**2. Robinhood's event-contract line: $173M in Q3.** Up more than 6x year on year, on a record 16.4B contracts; October added 5.9B. Non-sports was 30% of Q3 prediction volume, up from 15% ([Coinstrooper](https://coinstrooper.com/blog/house-memo-targets-officials-prediction-market-bets); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-17-robinhoods-non-sports-prediction-market-share-doubles-mizuho-442904)). Coinbase's figure is "$200M annualized," a different measure, so don't add them ([EconoTimes](https://econotimes.com/Coinbase-Prediction-Market-Revenue-Exceeds-200-Million-at-an-Annual-Rate-1778141)). *Why builders care:* distribution partners, not venues, are capturing a lot of the revenue.

**3. Polymarket says $1.8B annualised revenue.** Per Predicted, citing CNBC, up from $1B+ in June; Polymarket US did $14.91B notional in October, with combos 63.8% of last week's volume ([Predicted](https://newsletter.predicted.co/p/kalshi-65-billion-valuation-talks)). Separately, TokenSaga says its new taker-fee tiers start around 4.75% at a 50c price, roughly 36% above Kalshi's standard ([TokenSaga](https://tokensaga.com/polymarkets-new-taker-fee-tiers-push-costs-up-to-36-5-above-kalshi/)). *Why builders care:* fee gaps are an opening for routing and aggregator products.

**4. Pump.fun embeds Polymarket.** Live Nov 17 for permitted jurisdictions (not US, UK, France, Spain, Netherlands), including five-minute BTC markets. Pump.fun is a non-custodial interface and doesn't settle markets or hold funds: "不运营、结算或裁决任何市场" ([Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/zh-CN/newsflash/535457)). *Why builders care:* a template for front-ends that borrow a venue's liquidity.

**5. Kalshi's integrity committee and Martha Stewart.** Its independent committee's Q3 report (500M trades, ~900,000 alerts reviewed) found about 98% of volume carries lower insider-information risk. Kalshi will adopt category risk ratings and disclosed 50+ law-enforcement referrals in Q2-Q3 ([Kalshi News](https://news.kalshi.com/p/kalshi-market-integrity-committee-q3-report-martha-stewart)).

**6. Nvidia earnings tonight (new angle).** Kalshi's NVDA earnings-mention market showed $23.4M volume on its homepage; the call starts 5 PM ET with Colette Kress as interim CEO ([Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/)). Polymarket's "Jensen rejoins" event did ~$1.97M and "Fed decision in December" ~$1.56M in 24h at check ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/jensen-huang-rejoins-nvidia-byptptpt); [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/fed-decision-in-december-20260729232808632)).

**7. State and federal fronts.** Utah sued Stake over an alleged illegal online casino; a Texas bill would raise the trading age and limit event categories ([Covers timeline](https://www.covers.com/betting/prediction-sites/guides/prediction-markets-timeline)). The CFTC swap-rule comment period closes today; the federal response in the Wisconsin Seventh Circuit case is due Thursday ([Legal Sports Betting](https://www.legalsportsbetting.com/news/louisiana-s-order-on-kalshi-polymarket-expires-nov-22-11-17-2026/)).
