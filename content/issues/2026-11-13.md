**1. "Next CEO of NVIDIA?" is the breakout event market.** Nvidia announced on Nov 12 that Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO ([NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)). How "interim" vs "permanent" CEO resolves is the type of wording that sets off disputes.

Reuters, citing CME FedWatch, says traders see a December hike as close to certain ([Newsmetry/Reuters](https://newsmetry.xyz/en/news/nvidia-s-jensen-huang-to-step-down-as-ceo-us-index-futures-89828)). *Why builders care:* macro markets are a clean place to compare prediction-market prices with futures-implied odds.

## Build This

1. **Resolution-risk scanner for corporate-event markets.** "Next CEO" markets have to define interim vs. permanent, effective dates and announcement sources. A tool that scores rule wording and flags dispute risk before a shock would help both venues and traders. *Why now:* Nvidia's exit created a near-$1M/day market overnight, and "No Change" is still trading.
2. **State-compliance geofencing API.** Louisiana just became the 11th state to sue prediction markets, naming 10 platforms ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-12-louisiana-sues-kalshi-robinhood-other-prediction-markets-escalating-regulatory-fight-439052)). Coinbase is ending Ohio sports contracts by Nov 13 ([Predictable](https://predictable.gg/news/2026/11/10/coinbase-ohio-contracts-end-nov-13/)). Brokers and front-ends need a live per-state, per-contract-type availability feed.
3. **Self-certification filing copilot for DCMs.** The CFTC's July 24, 2026 rule bars broad template certifications, and Kalshi faces possible charges over filings ([Gate 新闻](https://www.gate.com/zh/news/detail/KALSHI/cftc-privately-tells-kalshi-it-may-sue-over-filings-for-dozens-of-new-25544399)). Gate puts the change as "不得通过宽泛、模板化的认证方式批量提交合约": no more batch-filing contracts under broad, templated certifications. With tZERO, STX and Sporttrade newly designated ([PM Pulse](https://pmpulse.substack.com/p/the-pm-pulse-086)), new exchanges need this from day one.
4. **Polymarket V2 migration linter.** CLOB query changes take effect Dec 14. The relayer narrowed its policy on Nov 11, and Protocol V2 rolls out in phases on Nov 23, Nov 24 and Dec 1 ([Polymarket changelog](https://docs.polymarket.com/changelog/predictions), [PM Pulse](https://pmpulse.substack.com/p/the-pm-pulse-086)). A CI check that flags deprecated calls in bot codebases could save many teams from silent failures.
5. **Combo/parlay analytics.** With roughly 75% of Kalshi's record weekend notional in Combos, there's room for pricing, hedging and risk dashboards built for multi-leg contracts.
