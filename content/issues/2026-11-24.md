# Kalshi Survives New York's TRO, Pauses Utah, and Supertake Raises $20M

*A Manhattan judge refused the immediate shutdown, a federal ruling pushed Kalshi out of Utah, and fresh seed money is flowing to builders.*

Checked Nov 23, 23:59 UTC / Nov 24, 07:59 China time.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. New York TRO denied.** Justice Melissa Crane denied the Attorney General's request for a temporary restraining order from the bench, so Kalshi keeps operating in New York while the case moves to a preliminary-injunction fight in January ([FinancialMarkets.com](https://www.financialmarkets.com/article/a-new-york-judge-refused-to-shut-down-kalshi-for-now-the-fight-over-a-preliminar), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/11/23/new-york-judge-declines-to-block-kalshi-from-operating-in-the-state-for-now)). Kalshi told the court it is open to excluding New York users under 21 ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/crypto/kalshi-faces-threat-of-nationwide-shutdown-in-new-york-case)). *Builders:* age gating is now a live product requirement.

**2. A "hybrid" regulation idea.** Crane floated that sports contracts might be gambling while financial derivatives stay federal. The AG's office claimed sports are 85% of Kalshi volume and financial contracts 1-2%, unverified ([Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/206314/new-york-judge-hybrid-kalshi-exchange-regulation)). *Builders:* contract-category tagging may decide where you can list.

**3. Kalshi pauses Utah.** After an adverse federal ruling, Kalshi paused operations in Utah ([Reuters via GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/11/23/kalshi-pauses-operations-in-utah-after-adverse-federal-court-ruling/)). 财联社: "Kalshi表示，因联邦法院作出不利终审判决，将暂停在犹他州的运营" ([Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202611243937745711.html)).

**4. CFTC geofencing deadline Dec 5.** Kalshi and Polymarket US have until Dec 5 to end domestic geographic restrictions or explain why they can't, while courts have ordered some of those blocks ([Gaming America](https://gamingamerica.com/news/1111083/kalshi-polymarket-prediction-market-geofencing-cftc-dec-5-deadline)). Separately, the CFTC's final prediction-market rule is under White House review ([Bloomberg via Aim Predict](https://www.aimpredict.com/zh-cn/news)).

**5. Who wins on Polymarket.** An Anti-Corruption Data Collective study: the top 1% of wallets (819) took 91% of about $23M in midterm congressional profits; 99% of wallets lost a combined $15M. Markets were about as accurate as election models (Senate 91%, House 95%) ([Reuters via StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/Most+winnings+on+Polymarket+midterm+bets+went+to+top+1%25+of+bettors%2C+report+says/27383864.html), [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/zh-CN/newsflash/537777)).

**6. Polymarket V2 goes live in stages.** Altcoin and weather markets first, a limited Bitcoin pilot Nov 24; V1 positions are not converted to V2 ([ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/article/2307445)).

**7. Wall Street wants the data.** Millennium Management is building tools to pull Polymarket and Kalshi prices as signals; it is not trading the contracts ([GMT Eight](https://gmteight.com/flash/detail/1577824)).
