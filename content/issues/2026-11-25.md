# Kalshi's Perps Out-Trade Its Prediction Markets as New York Counts Youth Losses

*Perpetual futures beat event contracts on Kalshi two days running, Utah dropped its criminal case, and NYC's Council wants executives under oath on Dec 14.*

Checked Nov 25, 00:05 UTC / 08:05 China time.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. Kalshi perps overtake event contracts.** Kalshi's perpetual futures did $3.4B notional on Thursday versus about $3B for prediction markets, then topped them again Friday, the first back-to-back days. Perps include leverage; event contracts are fully collateralized ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/kalshi-perps-volume-prediction-markets.html)). *Builders:* the exchange itself is becoming a derivatives venue, so the product edge is shifting to leverage-free, event-native tools.

**2. Utah drops the criminal case.** Attorney General Derek Brown dismissed charges against Kalshi and its two co-founders without prejudice after Kalshi paused Utah (about 177,000 users); they can be refiled if it returns ([Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/utah-drops-criminal-case-against-kalshi-after-prediction-market-exits-state-5048877)). CoinDesk: six other platforms agreed not to operate there ([CoinDesk 中文](https://www.coindesk.com/zh/policy/2026/11/24/utah-moves-to-drop-criminal-pursuit-of-kalshi-after-the-firm-halted-services-there)). *Builders:* "pause" is now a compliance playbook; build for clean wind-downs.

**3. NYC Council puts numbers on youth losses.** Speaker Julie Menin says Kalshi users aged 18 to 20 were about 10.5% of NYC accounts and took 17% of net losses, roughly $9M; Kalshi disputes the figures, saying LLC accounts were counted as institutional. Hearing Dec 14 with Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase, Gemini Titan and Novig ([區塊鏈周刊](https://chainweeks.com/news/182017.html), [iGaming Times](https://igaming-times.com/news/prediction-markets)). Chinese report: "个人用户亏损约5250万美元，机构账户盈利超过3.4亿美元" (retail down ~$52.5M, institutions up $340M+). *Builders:* deposit limits and marketing audit trails are product features.

**4. Kalshi x S&P Dow Jones Indices.** Event contracts will settle on official S&P DJI benchmark values, and the two will explore indexes built from Kalshi prices ([Coinsinsight](https://coinsinsight.com/1111869248/Kalshi-Signs-Agreement-With-SP-Dow-Jones-To-Expand-Event-Contracts)). *Builders:* prediction prices are becoming index inputs.

**5. Huang's return, 20x.** One Kalshi trader turned $42,633 into $878,680 on Jensen Huang returning as Nvidia CEO, implying roughly 5 cents entry; Polymarket hedging volume hit about $12.4M ([Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/zh/market-updates/huang-ren-xun-hui-gui-zhu-ka-si-li-jiao-yi-yuan-4-2-wan-mei-yuan-bian-87-8-wan-mei-yuan/)). Today's top Polymarket event by 24h volume is still "New Nvidia CEO named by Dec 31?" at about $3.7M ([Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?order=volume24hr&ascending=false&closed=false&limit=12)). *Builders:* corporate-event markets produce the sharpest information shocks.

**6. Tribal-land injunction.** A federal judge barred Kalshi sports contracts on four New Mexico reservations; a compliance report was due Nov 25 ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/covers-betting-news-digest-stories-you-need-to-know-today-nov-24-2026), [Prediction Markets Tracker](https://predictionmarketstracker.com/)). Polymarket US closed Michigan sports positions Nov 25 per the same tracker.

**7. Lakers sign Kalshi.** Kalshi is the team's Official Prediction Market Sponsor ([Lakers](https://www.nba.com/lakers/news/los-angeles-lakers-announce-partnership-with-kalshi)).
