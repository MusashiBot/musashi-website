## Today in Prediction Markets

- **Kalshi's pivot to hedging.** Kalshi says about 500 businesses have used it to hedge risks. Its BD director called the product "almost a form of micro-insurance". A New York rice importer, Not So Boring, is weighing roughly $9,000 for a contract paying $30,000 if tariffs hit 30% and $60,000 at 50%. Sports still dominate volume and 20+ states are suing. *Builders:* hedge-style contracts need pricing, underwriting and distribution layers that Kalshi won't build itself ([CoinDesk 中文](https://www.coindesk.com/zh/business/2026/11/27/kalshi-turns-to-small-businesses-as-legal-battles-threaten-forecasts-on-sports), [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/economy/markets/2026/kalshi-promotes-prediction-market-as-hedging-solution-for-small-businesses/)).
- **Polymarket US open interest ~$360M, up ~53% from Nov 20.** RootData: "Polymarket US 未平仓合约金额达 3.6 亿美元，一周增加约 53%" (open interest reached $360M). The regulated US venue is compounding while the offshore app is close-only for Americans ([RootData](https://cn.rootdata.com/news/847543), [CryptoVantage](https://www.cryptovantage.com/news/polymarket-vs-kalshi/)).
- **Kalshi overtakes Polymarket in US search.** Blask data: Kalshi brand searches rose 153% Sept to Oct for a 51.8% share vs Polymarket's 37.5%. October volume across eight US exchanges was a record $126.64B, with Kalshi at $98.15B (77.5%) and Polymarket US at $14.77B ([Sun Media 商传媒](https://sunmedia.tw/news/finance/1795734267-Kalshi%E7%BE%8E%E6%90%9C%E5%B0%8B%E7%86%B1%E5%BA%A6%E7%BF%BB%E5%80%8D%E3%80%80%E9%A6%96%E5%BA%A6%E8%B6%85%E8%BB%8APolymarket%E7%99%BB%E9%A0%82), [WPBarometer](https://wpbarometer.com/2026/11/27/kalshi-wins-the-search-race-but-its-trading-lead-is-even-wider/)).
- **Robinhood is routing around Kalshi.** Rothera, its Susquehanna JV, booked $28M event-contract revenue in its first full quarter; Robinhood expects most order flow to move to Rothera and Crypto.com by late 2026 or early 2027. Separately, a judge refused to send a Robinhood sports-contract class action to arbitration on Nov 27 ([Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/3034925/can-robinhood-s-rothera-expansion-reduce-its-reliance-on-kalshi), [iGaming Times](https://igaming-times.com/news/prediction-markets)).

Kalshi also faces a Nov 24 order blocking sports contracts on four New Mexico tribes' lands ([The Lines](https://www.thelines.com/prediction-markets/industry-news/utah-drops-kalshi-action-following-prediction-markets-exit/)).

KalshiEX) has Kalshi's response due Dec 7; NYC Council wants Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase, Gemini Titan and Novig to testify Dec 14 on youth marketing ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/opinion/finance/6240695-utah-kalshi-sports-betting-lawsuit/), [Cryptocasinos](https://cryptocasinos.com/news/legal-regulation/nyc-council-kalshi-polymarket-testify-marketing/)).

## Build This

1. **Hedging desk for small business.** Wrap Kalshi's tariff/weather/commodity contracts with quotes in dollars of exposure, not contract counts. *Why now:* Kalshi is courting ~500 SMBs and needs distribution.
2. **State-by-state compliance map as an API.** Feeds, not articles: which venues can serve which ZIP, with court status. *Why now:* Utah, Louisiana, New Mexico, Michigan and NJ's SCOTUS petition all moved this week.
3. **Order-flow router across Kalshi, Rothera, Crypto.com, Polymarket US.** *Why now:* Robinhood says flow is shifting venues by early 2027.
4. **Search and attention dashboard for venues.** Blask shows brand share flipping; combine with volume to forecast share shifts.
5. **Reference-price layer for funds.** Millennium-style desks want clean cross-venue signals (see Furnace below).
