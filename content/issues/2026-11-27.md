# Congress Widens Its Insider-Trading Probe to Hyperliquid and PredictIt, as Millennium Mines Prediction Prices

*The House Oversight probe leaves Kalshi and Polymarket behind, a multi-strategy hedge fund builds signal tools from prediction data, and OmenX and Rialto raise seeds.*

*Checked Nov 27, 2026, 00:00-08:00 China time (Nov 26, 16:00 UTC onward). Context for builders, not betting advice.*

## Today in Prediction Markets

- **House Oversight expands its insider-trading probe (Nov 26-27).** Chairman James Comer is now demanding documents from Hyperliquid Labs, Crypto.com and Aristotle Exchange (PredictIt's parent) on customer identity checks, suspicious-trade detection and reporting. The May probe of Kalshi and Polymarket has produced nearly 1,000 documents and 5 briefings. The trigger includes a Hyperliquid account that opened a large leveraged short minutes before an undisclosed tariff announcement. Builders: surveillance and KYC are now a cross-venue expectation, onchain venues included ([GMT Eight](https://gmteight.com/flash/detail/1536077)).
- **Millennium Management is building tools to read Polymarket and Kalshi as signals** for traditional assets, per people familiar. It is not trading the contracts; PMs are interested in geopolitical, economic and commodity outcomes. Builders: the buyer for clean prediction-price feeds is a multi-strategy hedge fund ([GMT Eight](https://www.gmt8press.com/flash/detail/1577824)).
- **Kalshi walks away from Louisiana.** It filed a voluntary dismissal without prejudice of its federal suit, within a month of filing, after the state's Nov 12 TRO against platforms. Why is unclear. Together with Utah, this is a pattern of retreat-and-refile ([WBRZ](https://www.wbrz.com/news/kalshi-files-to-voluntarily-dismiss-its-lawsuit-against-louisiana)).
- **New York's judge declines the TRO.** Judge Melissa Crane refused AG Letitia James's request but pushed for limits on sports contracts for users under 21; a January hearing follows ([The Lines](https://www.thelines.com/prediction-markets/industry-news/kalshi-granted-reprieve-in-ny-legal-case/)).
- **Oregon eyes an ~8% tax for 2027.** Senate Majority Leader Anthony Broadman says he will likely seek about 8% to fund wildfire costs; Oregon collects nothing today ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/11/oregon-lawmakers-eye-8-tax-on-kalshi-style-prediction-markets-to-fund-wildfire-costs/)). Per-state tax rates (NY bill at 10%, Oregon ~8%) are becoming a product input.
- **Compute markets: thin history, big curve.** A Predicted newsletter notes earlier compute contracts fell to $807K volume in September and $60K in October (down 98% from August) before Polymarket's forward curve launched ([Predicted](https://newsletter.predicted.co/p/compute-prediction-markets-opportunity)). Liquidity, not listing, is the open problem.
- **Polymarket volume, checked ~08:00 China time.** Top 24h events: Eagles vs. Cowboys ~$4.4M, Bitcoin price on Nov 27 ~$2.1M, "New NVIDIA CEO named by Dec 31?" ~$1.9M, Fed December ~$1.2M. The Nvidia market fell from ~$5.4M yesterday ([Gamma API](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?order=volume24hr&ascending=false&closed=false&limit=12)).

## Build This

1. **Insider-trading surveillance for any venue.** Wallet-clustering and pre-announcement-trade alerts, sold to exchanges and to regulators' vendors. *Why now:* Comer's probe now covers onchain and regulated venues alike.
2. **Prediction-signal terminal for multi-strat funds.** Normalized, timestamped feeds with resolution-rule metadata. *Why now:* Millennium is building this in-house; smaller funds can't.
3. **State tax and licensing calculator.** Per-state tax (NY bill 10%, Oregon ~8%) and legal status as an API for front-ends. *Why now:* two states moved this week.
4. **Compute-market liquidity layer.** Market-making and RFQ for long-dated GPU contracts. *Why now:* prior compute markets lost 98% of volume in two months.
5. **Retry-aware compliance workflow.** Dismiss-and-refile tracking for operators facing multi-state suits. *Why now:* Utah and Louisiana both ended "without prejudice."
