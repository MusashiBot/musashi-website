# Kalshi Did $78.8B in November, Polymarket Rewrote Its Fees, and Kalshi Plans Stocks

*A 70% API rebate at Polymarket US, category-based fee cuts, Kalshi's stock-and-ETF plans, and a New York hearing that lands today.*

Checked Nov 22, 2026, ~16:00 UTC / Nov 23, 00:00 UTC (08:00 China time). Items from prior briefs appear only with new developments.

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. November volume: Kalshi $78.82B, Polymarket US $10.62B, Polymarket $3.24B.** Month-to-date snapshot published Nov 22, 1:28 a.m. ET ([CryptoInfo](https://cryptoinfo.ch/kalshi-leads-prediction-markets-with-78-8b-volume-in-november/)). Kalshi's figure is contract face value, so it is not like-for-like with USD-traded venues. *Builders:* Kalshi is roughly 7x Polymarket US, and the gap is the fight for flow.

**2. Polymarket US launches a 70% taker-fee rebate for API trades.** Temporary, through Dec 31, 2026, aimed at algorithmic traders; app users keep 0-30% volume rebates. Polymarket US did $3.73B in trailing seven-day volume to Nov 18 ([PMTools](https://www.predictionmarket.tools/news/prediction-market-news-2026-11-22-morning)). *Builders:* bots, market makers and routers get paid to integrate this winter.

**3. Polymarket repriced fees by category.** Effective 6:59 p.m. ET Friday: crypto, economics, culture, weather and non-election politics cut to 0.01; election, finance and technology raised to 0.05 ([FinancialMarkets.com](https://www.financialmarkets.com/article/polymarket-repriced-its-fees-by-category-as-kalshis-court-calendar-fills-up)). *Builders:* fee-aware routing and cost simulators now need per-category logic.

**4. Kalshi plans stock and ETF trading within two months,** via its July acquisition of brokerage FB Securities, plus AI compute futures and the $65B raise talks ([GMT Eight](https://gmteight.com/flash/detail/1611452)). *Builders:* the incumbent is becoming a multi-asset exchange; pure-play apps need a niche.

**5. New York hearing today.** Justice Melissa Crane of New York County Supreme Court considers the AG's proposed order, which Kalshi says could halt it nationwide; Judge Schofield denied the CFTC's emergency bid Nov 20 ([FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/federal-judge-rejects-cftcs-second-bid-to-block-new-yorks-kalshi-lawsuit/)). Louisiana must say by Nov 24 whether it will seek to return its case to state court ([FinancialMarkets.com](https://www.financialmarkets.com/article/polymarket-repriced-its-fees-by-category-as-kalshis-court-calendar-fills-up)).

**6. Oregon eyes a prediction-market tax.** Senate Majority Leader Anthony Broadman may push about 8%, modeled on North Carolina's 6% levy on net sports-contract fee revenue from Jan 1, 2027 ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/oregon-prediction-market-tax-broadman-fragala-2027-session)). *Builders:* state-by-state tax accounting is becoming a compliance product.

**7. Senate's DEATH BETS Act** (passed Nov 19 by voice vote) bars CFTC-registered exchanges from war, terrorism, assassination and death contracts; the House companion H.R. 7942 sits in subcommittee ([Mempool Brief](https://mempoolbrief.com/articles/senate-votes-to-ban-war-and-death-contracts-on-kalshi-polymarket)).
