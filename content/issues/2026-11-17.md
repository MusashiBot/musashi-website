*Senate Democrats say reverse it, Louisiana wants its case back in state court, and Kalshi says stocks and ETFs are two months out.*

## Today in Prediction Markets

**1. The 30-day geofence ultimatum.** The CFTC told its regulated platforms that restricting service state by state breaks federal derivatives law, and gave them 30 days to drop the restrictions or explain why they can't. On Monday, Senate Agriculture Committee Democrats (Schiff and Slotkin among them) wrote that the directive "directly conflicts with active court orders... and must be reversed." Courts have ordered platforms to stop contracts in Michigan, Nevada and Washington and on two California tribes' land ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/11/16/u-s-senate-democrats-tell-cftc-to-back-off-its-demand-prediction-markets-defy-bans)). *Why builders care:* every venue now has to pick between a federal order and local court orders. Geofencing, once a checkbox, is a product.

**2. Louisiana wants out of federal court.** The AG's office filed an emergency motion to remand on Monday, calling Polymarket's removal "an egregious abuse of the federal judicial system." It asked for a ruling before its own Dec. 9 submission date ([RG](https://rg.org/news/prediction-markets/louisiana-emergency-motion-remand-kalshi-polymarket-federal-court)). Gate adds the TRO expires Nov 22 unless extended: "该 TRO ... 将于 11 月 22 日到期，除非获得延期" ([Gate](https://www.gate.com/zh/news/detail/robinhood-and-rothera-file-motion-to-overturn-louisiana-prediction-market-25655502)). The injunction hearing is Nov 30.

**3. Kalshi becomes a stock exchange.** CLS reports Kalshi will launch stock and ETF trading within two months: "事件预测平台Kalshi将在未来两个月内推出股票和ETF交易" ([CLS](https://api3.cls.cn/b/2536022)). FXBus says it closed the FB Securities broker acquisition in July and is still in talks on a $65B round ([FXBus](https://www.fxbus.com/cn/news/20261117035048339712.html)). *Why builders care:* a venue that offers events, perps and equities in one account squeezes any app built on one of them.

**4. CFTC probes both leaders.** Politico: the CFTC is running separate probes into Kalshi (self-certification of dozens of contracts) and Polymarket (whether it excluded US users under a 2022 settlement) while defending the industry in court ([Politico](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/16/prediction-markets-probes-selig-01166685); [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/cftc-investigates-kalshi-polymarket-prediction-markets-1832581)). Today the Senate Agriculture Committee holds a confirmation hearing for CFTC nominees Anonick and Rowland ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/11/16/u-s-senate-democrats-tell-cftc-to-back-off-its-demand-prediction-markets-defy-bans)).

**5. Where the money is: short-dated commodities.** Kalshi plus Polymarket's international exchange did **$543.0M** of financial-contract notional Nov 9-16. Commodities were $389.2M, of which **$375.9M** was in contracts lasting 24 hours or less. The biggest longer-dated market was "Fed Decision in December?" at $17.9M ([The Midpoint](https://themidpointnews.com/news/prediction-markets-weekly-2026-w46/)). Polymarket's Fed December market did ~$1.1M in 24h at check ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/fed-decision-in-december-20260729232808632)).

**6. Health contracts go live.** Both Polymarket and Kalshi now list contracts such as whether the CDC will change vaccine recommendations; critics warn about trial participants altering behavior ([GamblingNews](https://www.gamblingnews.com/news/polymarket-and-kalshi-offer-health-related-prediction-contracts/)).

**7. Search-demand split.** Polymarket's 30-day volume was $5.5B in contracts ($2.7B paid), 4% of tracked prediction-market volume; Kalshi was 76% and Polymarket US 11% ([DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/volume/polymarket/), Nov 15, 9:30 PM ET).
